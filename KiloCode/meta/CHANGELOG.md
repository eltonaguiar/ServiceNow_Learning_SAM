## Design Principles

1. **Learn by doing** - Every concept has a lab
2. **5-step loop** - Orient, Configure, Build, Verify, Teach Back
3. **Under 2 hours** - Each module should be learnable in under 2 hours
4. **Beginner-friendly** - ELI5 terminology, no jargon without explanation
5. **Practical** - Focus on what you can actually do, not just theory
