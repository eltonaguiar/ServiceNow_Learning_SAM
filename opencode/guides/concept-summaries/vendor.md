# Vendor — SAM Concept Summary

*Generated: 2026-11-04T23:29:38.096976+00:00*
*Table: alm_vendor*

## ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5)

A vendor is the company you buy assets from — like Dell, Apple, Microsoft, or your local IT supplier. You need vendor records so ServiceNow knows who to call for support or warranty claims.

## Technical Detail

Vendors are stored in the alm_vendor table. Each vendor record includes contact information and can be linked to assets, contracts, and purchase orders.

## Where to Find It in ServiceNow

Asset Management > Vendors > All

## Key Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Vendor | Company that sells or supports assets |
| Vendor Contact | Person to contact at the vendor |
