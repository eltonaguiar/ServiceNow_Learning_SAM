# Asset Tag — SAM Concept Summary

*Generated: 2026-11-04T23:29:38.069032+00:00*
*Table: alm_asset*

## ELI5 (Explain Like I'm 5)

An asset tag is like a name sticker on a lunchbox. It's a unique number (like AST-001234) that you stick on each physical asset so you can match it to its record in ServiceNow.

## Technical Detail

The asset tag is stored in the asset_tag field on alm_asset. It should be unique across all assets and is often used for barcode scanning during audits.

## Where to Find It in ServiceNow

Asset Management > Assets > All > Asset Tag field

| Asset Tag | Unique identifier sticker on physical asset |
| Barcode | Machine-readable version of asset tag |
