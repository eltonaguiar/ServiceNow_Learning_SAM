# SAM Concepts — ELI5 Reference

## What Is an Asset?

An asset is anything your company owns that's IT-related and worth tracking:
- **Hardware:** Laptops, servers, monitors, phones, printers
- **Software:** Microsoft Office, Adobe Creative Cloud, antivirus
- **Contracts:** Maintenance agreements, support contracts, leases
- **Licenses:** Permission to use software (per user, per device, enterprise)

## What Is the Asset Lifecycle?

The journey an asset takes from "we need this" to "we're getting rid of this":

1. **Request** — Someone needs something
2. **Procure** — You buy it
3. **Receive** — It arrives
4. **Deploy** — You set it up and give it to someone
5. **Maintain** — You fix it, update it, track it
6. **Retire** — It's old or broken, you dispose of it

## What Is an Asset Tag?

A unique sticker number on each physical asset (like "AST-001234"). It's how you match the physical laptop in your hand to the digital record in ServiceNow.

## What Is a Category?

Categories organize assets into groups. "Laptop" is different from "Server" which is different from "Software License." Each category shows different fields.

## What Is a State?

State tells you where the asset is in its journey:
- **In Stock:** Sitting in the warehouse, available
- **Assigned:** Given to an employee
- **In Repair:** Being fixed
- **Retired:** No longer used, disposed of

## What Is Software License Management?

Tracking how many copies of software you're allowed to use vs. how many you're actually using. Prevents paying for unused licenses or getting fined for using too many.

## What Is Contract Management?

Tracking agreements with vendors — when they start, when they expire, how much they cost, what they cover. Prevents surprise expirations.

## Quick Reference: 5 Questions to Ask About Any Asset

1. What is it? (Category)
2. Who has it? (Assigned To)
3. Where is it? (Location)
4. When was it bought? (Purchase Date)
5. When does it expire/need replacement? (Warranty/Retirement Date)
